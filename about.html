About R’s Story

Travel, Design & Little Joys.

R’s Story is an online magazine about travel, design, beautiful streets, cafés and the small discoveries of everyday life.

Through photographs, films and letters, it hopes to offer a little inspiration — so that you might enjoy your own story a little more, wherever you are.
